Rolled Skin Guide

1. RelaxTake balls from fridge for 20–30 min.
2. RollRoll to 12 in / 30.5 cm and 2–3 mm thick.
3. DockDock thoroughly, especially the centre.
4. Uncovered dry
5. Skin checkUse the actual feel of the dough before covering.
SOFT / TACKY Keep uncovered in the fridge.
DRY / FIRM Loosely cover and continue the cold cure.
VERY DRY / BRITTLE Cover now. Don't keep drying.
